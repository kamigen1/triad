Triad Summons: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Triad Summons is a free card game. This page says what the game stores about you, why, and how to remove it. We keep it short because there is not much to say: no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

What we store

  • Your account name, the name friends find you by.
  • Your sign-in. If you use a password, only a salted, hashed version is kept, never the password itself. If you use Google sign-in, we keep Google's anonymous account identifier for your account. We do not receive or store your email address, and we never see your Google password.
  • A recovery code hash, only if you choose to make one, so a forgotten password can be reset.
  • Your game progress: cards, coins, decks, story progress, match results and your friends list, so it follows you between phones.
  • Technical error reports: the game version, an error message and your browser type, to fix bugs. They carry no name.

What we do not do

  • We show no ads and use no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone, except the services that run the game (below).
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Who handles the data

The game's data is stored on Cloudflare (the game server and its database). The website is served by GitHub Pages. If you use Google sign-in, Google handles that step under its own policy.

Friends and names

Other players can see your account name, whether you are online, and the profile details the game shows (such as level and rank). Nothing else is visible to them.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Account and data → Delete account in the game, or use the account deletion page. Deleting removes your account, saved game, friends and recovery code from our server. Error reports contain no name, so they cannot be linked back to you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, tell us (below) and we will remove it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions or removal requests: open an issue at github.com/kamigen1/triad/issues.
